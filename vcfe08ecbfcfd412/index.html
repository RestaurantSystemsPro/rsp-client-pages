Mondays get a bar 38% above anything they have actually done since June, so Monday prints a miss every week however the shift runs. Saturdays get a bar 28% under trend, so Saturday prints a beat every week even on a poor night. The two errors cancel in the weekly total, which is why the aggregate looks clean, but the daily flags will be wrong in both directions and the owner learns to discount them.

Three days also import last year’s one-off events as targets: Wed 8/26 at $5,138.77 is 1.53x the trailing Wednesday; Thu 9/3 at $7,447.85 is 2.09x the trailing Thursday, off a $9,309.81 day last year; Mon 9/21 at $4,708.19 is 1.95x the trailing Monday, off a $5,885.24 day. Unless something real is booked on those dates, all three are guaranteed false misses, and 9/3 will read as a catastrophe.

Suggested fix, same total: keep the $131,351 period target but distribute it on this year’s trailing day-of-week shares instead of last year’s, then hand-set the three event days to what is actually on the books. The revenue goal survives intact and a daily miss starts meaning something again.
