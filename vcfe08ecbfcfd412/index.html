Cash

HIGHThree nights of cash have gone into the safe uncounted
The end of day report says exactly how much cash should have been in the bag each night: $850.66 Wednesday, $496.33 Thursday and $497.40 Friday. That is $1,844.39 over three nights. On none of those three nights did a manager count the deposit, enter it, or confirm it went to the bank. Until somebody counts it there is no way to tell an intact deposit from a short one. The over and short figure on those days is the uncounted deposit itself, not a till error. This is the single control that protects the owner, and it is the one that has lapsed.
HIGHPaid out log empty three days running, including Friday night
Wednesday, Thursday and Friday all show no paid outs at all. An empty paid out log on a Friday, sitting next to an uncounted deposit, is the exact combination that lets cash leave the building without a record. On Tuesday there was a $150.00 cash paid out for bingo entertainment, and the receipt field on it holds a date rather than a receipt number. Entertainment, cleaning crews and bonuses paid in cash need a receipt number every time.
MEDIUMTuesday's counted deposit was not banked that day
Joseph Raven counted $734.00 on Tuesday, which is exactly right. The answer to whether it went to the bank that day, by itself, was no. Deposits that sit and then get banked together are the hardest kind to trace back.
