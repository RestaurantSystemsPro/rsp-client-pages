High
Deposits have not gone to the bank for four straight days. Up to $2,114.00 is sitting.
October 26 ($519.00, counted by Derek), October 27 ($485.00, counted by Joseph Raven) and October 29 ($639.00, counted by Joseph Raven) were each answered "no" to being banked that day, with no reason given. That is $1,643.00 confirmed as held. October 28 adds a further $471.00 with no log behind it, so as much as $2,114.00 may be on site.
