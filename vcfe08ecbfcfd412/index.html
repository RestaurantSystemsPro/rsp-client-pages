Setup items that unlock the reports

These are the store side steps that make the cost and usage numbers above trustworthy. Each one closes a specific hole seen in this week's figures.

  • Assign an inventory location to all 27 open products. This is the single highest value item on the list. It is the direct cause of a 93.44% prime cost reading against a 66.5% underlying reality.
  • Generate ideal cost. Until ideal usage is set, the usage report cannot show a variance at all, only actual usage restated. There is no way to prove or disprove an overage without it.
  • Upload invoice photos the day the delivery arrives. A purchase counted on the shelf with no invoice in the system produces a negative usage line, which cancels a real overage somewhere else and hides both.
  • Enter the counted deposit against the day. Counting it and writing it in the log is most of the work. Entering it is what closes the day.
  • Schedule people in the department they clock into. Managers clocking as hourly manager while scheduled as bartender or server moves real hours between front and back of house and makes both budgets read incorrectly.
  • Value the waste sheets weekly. Four weeks of zeros means every dollar of waste is currently landing in cost as though it were sold.
